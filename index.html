How points work

Wake points

Study bonus

A flat +3 for 30 minutes or more. It does not scale with the streak and does not depend on the wake time — studying and waking early count separately.

Streak multiplier

Multiplies the wake points only, never the study bonus. Days 1–2 count at 1x, days 3–6 at 1.25x, and day 7 onward at 1.5x.

Keeping the streak

Every logged day moves it up one. A missed weekday softens it back one tier rather than wiping it — a 7-day streak becomes 4, a 3-to-6-day streak becomes 1. Weekends are free: logging one extends the streak, skipping one costs nothing.

Waking at 6:45 on the fourth day of a streak, with 40 minutes of study: 4 wake points × 1.25 = 5, plus 3 for the study, so 8 points that day.

Corrections

Nothing is scored once and stored. Every point on this page is recalculated from the logged times each time it loads, so fixing a wrong entry from last week updates the streak and every day after it.

Sync across devices

Optional. A sync code keeps the same history on your phone and any other browser you open this in. Anyone who has the code can read and change the log, so share it like a password.

Backup

Data lives in this browser only. Save a backup file now and then so a cleared browser or a new phone doesn't lose the streak.
